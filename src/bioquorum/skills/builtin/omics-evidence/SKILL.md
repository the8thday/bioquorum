---
name: omics-evidence
description: Analyze omics measurements with documented inputs, biological replication, assay scale and reproducible contrasts.
---
Inspect the supplied input, feature identifiers, assay and measurement layer. Report whether counts, normalized intensities, compositional measurements or synthetic examples are available. Record the file and analysis run.

Identify the donor, sample, batch, condition and repeated measurements. Check the design table before testing a contrast. Determine where pairs are missing and where condition and batch are inseparable. Preserve author-supplied annotations and document any regrouping.

Choose quality checks relevant to the assay. Report their denominators and assess sensitivity to thresholds that affect the result. Compare biological replicates on a justified scale; hundreds of cells from one donor supply one donor. Distinguish pooled estimates from equal-weight sample summaries. Inspect whether a small sample, cell class or outlier drives a contrast.

Bind reported measurements to the run and record the design limitations. An expression measurement may motivate a functional experiment; it does not measure treatment response or absolute surface density. Consult accession metadata, study methods and accompanying sample tables when the project's permissions allow.
