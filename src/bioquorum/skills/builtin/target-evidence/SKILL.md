---
name: target-evidence
description: Evaluate a therapeutic target by connecting human biology, intervention direction, efficacy and safety to a specific research decision.
---
Identify the disease, population, proposed intervention and decision. Select the categories that could change that decision. Explain why a category is out of scope when relevant.

- Look for human genetic or perturbation evidence. Check the allele, phenotype, ancestry and direction against a primary study. A gene association alone does not identify a therapeutic direction.
- Inspect disease and comparator tissue, cell identity and protein localization. Separate RNA detection from accessible surface protein and achievable exposure.
- Relate pathway biology to selective intervention. Check which molecule a drug actually engages, its modality and whether a comparator isolates its contribution.
- Read a primary trial report for patient endpoints, a registry for study status, and a regulatory label for an approved indication or safety restriction. Record the population, comparator and follow-up.
- Retrieve counterevidence, safety observations and missing tissue or patient evidence. A title or registry entry is a lead. Document which record was read and which decision its result changes.

Use the enabled sources actually available in this project. Typical starting points are Open Targets, GWAS Catalog, HPA, PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov and regulatory labels. A timeout is an unresolved query.
