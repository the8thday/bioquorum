---
name: evidence-synthesis
description: Build a focused review of competing scientific claims, study designs, endpoints and uncertainty.
---
State the population, intervention or exposure, comparator, outcome and time horizon. Choose a search scope and record the exact source and terms. Search both for primary results and for evidence that could reverse the leading interpretation.

Read methods and results wherever accessible. A title, study registration, abstract and full report support different claims. Match records by study identifier and population so that multiple reports of one cohort are not counted as independent replications. Preserve setting, selection, comparator, endpoint and follow-up for each line of evidence.

Compare observational studies, randomized comparisons and mechanistic measurements on their own terms. Inspect neutral findings and harms, uncertainty, missing follow-up and differences in eligibility. Distinguish an inconclusive comparison from evidence of equivalence. Scope any negative search result to the actual query. Useful sources include study registries, PubMed or Europe PMC and regulatory assessments when available.
